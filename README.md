# Swapp — Learn & Teach 🎓

A campus skill-exchange app where students trade what they know for what they want to learn — no cash needed, just time and credits.

🔗 **Live demo:** https://atiyarizwan249-cpu.github.io/Swapp-campus-app/

## The Problem

Students have valuable skills (coding, music, photography, languages) but no easy way to trade them with peers on campus. Paid tutoring is expensive, and there's no structured space for informal peer-to-peer learning.

## The Solution

Swapp lets students:
- **Browse skills** other students are offering and request a swap using time-credits
- **Post in the Forum** to find swap partners, ask questions, or offer group sessions
- **Rate swaps** after they're completed, building trust through visible ratings
- **Track a streak** to stay motivated and keep learning consistently
- **Sign up with a school/college email only**, keeping the community verified and campus-specific

## Monetization Model (Freemium)

- **Free tier:** 3 swaps/month, standard matching
- **Premium ($4/month):** unlimited swaps, priority matching, verified certificates
- **Semester Pass:** discounted long-term plan (~17% off)
- **GPA-based discount:** students with a 3.5+ GPA get 20% off Premium
- **Buy extra credits:** starting at $15 for students who run out mid-semester

## Tech Stack

- HTML, CSS, JavaScript (single-file frontend prototype)
- Hosted for free on GitHub Pages
- Data currently stored in browser localStorage (demo purposes)

## Roadmap

- Real backend (Firebase) for shared, multi-user data across the forum and credits system
- Real email verification for school/college sign-ups
- Smart skill-matching suggestions
- Paid group workshops hosted by Premium users
